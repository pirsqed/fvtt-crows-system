# Playing the playtest

Before playing, the GM should review [player permissions and loot setup](README.md#gm-setup-player-permissions).

The system handles rolls and the basic resource bookkeeping. Keep the rules and item descriptions handy for these table decisions:

- **Weapon qualities:** qualities appear in attack chat. Apply their special effects yourself. Adjust the damage amount in the allocation dialog for effects such as Brutal before confirming it.
- **Ammunition and thrown weapons:** track ammunition, reloads, and retrieval manually.
- **Critical hits, doom, and conditions:** resolve extra actions, counterattacks, positioning, and condition effects at the table.
- **Spellbooks:** enable **Spellbook** in an equipment item's settings, then use **Cast** from the item or inventory. Set its usage dice and tier effects in the item settings. Cast rolls Mind and allows expertise afterward. Follow the chat reminder for chaos/backlash and usage dice; spell effects, targets, durations, and trait exceptions are manual. Keep the book in hand when required by the rules.
- **Wounds:** overflow damage fills the first available wound slots. Move wounds by clearing their old slots and marking the desired slots. Human and Animal NPCs use their configured inventory slot count; set Type to Human or Animal and configure Slots. A wounded slot holding equipment reduces speed by 1. All slots wounded means death.
- **XP:** on the Crow's Traits tab, enter all earned XP as **Total XP (TXP)** and purchases as **Spent XP**. Available XP is the difference. Spending never lowers TXP. Apply trait purchases and advancement benefits manually after resting.
- **Resources and downtime:** check usage dice, food, rest benefits, repairs, travel, crafting, and village changes against the rules; buttons apply only their stated actions.

Shields must be in a hand to provide AD. Moving one into storage unequips it; equip it again after returning it to a hand. Custom or renamed shields should have the **Shield (hand only)** checkbox enabled.

Existing owned spellbooks can be enabled with their new checkbox. Newly built spellbook compendium entries include it automatically; rebuilding compendiums does not change owned items.
