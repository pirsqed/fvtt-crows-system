Your Crows playtest PDFs can go here! :)

Extract the ZIP from your MCDM playtest download on the computer where you use
Foundry in a browser. Keep the packet's original filenames and subfolders.
For a complete import, include the Characters book, Ref book, core Inventory
Cards, Profession cards, and POI/Dungeon cards. Keep one copy of each PDF.

In your world, enable Crows PDF Importer. Open Settings >
Configure Settings > Crows > Import Playtest Content. Choose
Packet folder to select this folder and its subfolders, or Individual PDFs to
select the books directly. Check the detected book assignments, extract, choose
entries, review changes, and import as the active GM.

The importer reads your PDFs in the browser. For a hosted game, select the
packet on your own computer; you do not need to put it on the Foundry server.
Only reviewed content is saved to the world. Your original PDFs and packet are
not included in this system's release ZIP.

For a walkthrough, open Settings > Configure Settings > Crows >
Start Here, or read the system README.
